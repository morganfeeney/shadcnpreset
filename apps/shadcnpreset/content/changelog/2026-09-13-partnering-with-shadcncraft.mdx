shadcnpreset is now partnering with [shadcncraft](https://shadcncraft.com?atp=shadcnpreset&src=changelog). They make well-built shadcn/ui blocks for real products, and seeing a preset applied to them is one of the quickest ways to judge how it will hold up in a real project.

What's included:

- **Marketing** — a full landing page with hero, logos, benefits, metrics, testimonial, pricing, FAQs and footer
- **Application** — a workspace overview with metric cards, charts, a members table and an activity feed
- **Store** — categories, products, a product page, cart and checkout
- **Chat** — an AI conversation where the assistant generates UI inline, showing how a preset carries through to generative UI

Each preview is built from the components defined by the preset, so switching presets restyles the whole page: colours, radius, fonts and icons.

Links to shadcncraft in this entry and in the previews are affiliate links. If you buy through them, shadcnpreset earns a commission at no extra cost to you.
