[shadcn themes](/themes) is a new section with 60 free themes for shadcn/ui, taken from the most voted community presets. A theme is the colour layer of a preset: colours, radius and charts. Your components, style and icons stay as they are.

Every theme has its own page:

- **See it on real UI** — cards, forms, charts and a dropdown, in whichever mode you're browsing in
- **Every colour** — the palette, token by token
- **Copy the CSS** — or apply it with `npx shadcn@latest apply --preset <code> --only theme`
- **Real blocks** — a landing page built from [shadcncraft](https://shadcncraft.com?atp=shadcnpreset&sub1=changelog) Pro blocks, dressed in the theme, so you can see it beyond a few components
- **Browse without losing your place** — the sidebar lists every theme with search and a base colour filter, and switching keeps you where you were on the page

Themes come without fonts, so you can now add a pairing. Open **Fonts** on any theme page and pick one of the pairings community presets use, with the ones used alongside that theme first, or choose any heading and body font yourself. The previews switch to your fonts straight away, and the CSS and command include them: `--only theme,font` installs the fonts and sets them up. The pairing is kept in the link, so you can share it, and it stays with you as you switch themes.

Also new: a themes rail on the homepage, and a [side-by-side guide to the eight shadcn styles](/learn/how-to-choose-a-shadcn-style), now titled for the styles it compares.
