[Describe a preset](/describe) uses [Jev from TypeSafe](https://typesafe.ai) to turn a few words into a preset. Type "cosy coffee shop" or "brutalist, sharp, black and white" and the preview restyles itself while you are still typing: colours, fonts, icons, radius and the shell, all at once.

What's included:

- **Live results** — a new preset arrives about half a second after you stop typing, rendered in the same shadcn create preview as every preset page
- **See what Jev understood** — the sidebar shows each part of the preset, how sure Jev was, and whether you asked for it or it was inferred from the overall mood
- **Ideas to start from** — one click on an idea fills in a description to try or adapt
- **Keep what you like** — open the result as a full preset page, vote for it, copy its code or share it

Jev is built to make fast, structured decisions rather than write text. It reads your description once and picks every part of the preset in a single request, which is why results come back so quickly.
