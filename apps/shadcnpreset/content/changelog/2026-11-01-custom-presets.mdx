A custom preset is a whole shadcn preset you shape yourself. Start from any preset in [Ask AI](/assistant) and change its style, fonts, icons, radius and colours, by chatting or with shadcn create's pickers. It can also use your exact brand colours, like `#1E3A8A` or `oklch(70.4% .191 22.216)`, which a preset code can't hold. Mention a colour in the chat and choose **Use my colours**.

What's included:

- **Style** — the eight shadcn styles, which set the spacing, padding and shape of every component
- **Fonts** — a body font and a heading font, or one for both
- **Icons and radius** — any of the five icon libraries and five radius sizes
- **Colours** — Base Color, Theme and Chart Color each take a list choice or one custom colour, carried through the preset the way shadcn does, and you can override single tokens like the background
- **Edit by chatting** — while the preset is open beside the chat, the chat only works on it: "switch to Mira", "use Lora for headings", "make the theme green"
- **Install it** — set up the preset with its code, then add your colours with one `npx shadcn@latest add` command, or copy them as CSS
- **Share it** — every custom preset has its own page

Also fixed: two buttons in the preset preview ignored the preset's style, so in Sera they stayed rounded and in normal case.
